Encrypted GPT-2 decode through all 12 blocks with the encrypted argmax, 16 teacher-forced
positions, two isolated sessions per system. \textit{Planned}: refreshes the plan places
(cut sites and hint-triggered); \textit{executed}: bootstraps per token, including the 23
deliberate ones the model runs. \textit{e2e}: steady-state wall time per token, mean with its
95\% interval. \textit{KL}: median over positions of the divergence between the encrypted and
the plaintext next-token distributions; \textit{top-1}: agreement with the plaintext argmax.
DaCapo and Orion are the released tools' site selections, unpruned; every baseline runs with
our correction factors and sparse routing, 48 levels. $^{\star}$Our reimplementation, pruned
by its published post-pass: the released tool produces no plan on these graphs.
$^{\dagger}$Misses the reference-rank gate at one position (rank 9, limit 8).
